## Assessment

Four queries return only quality hits (Power/comms, Foraging, Food/water, Solar). Seven are mixed—at least one solid hit per query but clutter from off-topic results (Canning education duplicate, Medical history volume, Emergency training manual plumbing sidebar, Fire shelter nuclear weapons context, Skills practice social skills detour, Medical emergency tort entry). Two queries are outright failures: "AI assistants" returns zero relevant hits (search misrouted to old shorthand and obstetrics), and "Maps that give directions" finds no navigation resources.

Seven N/A queries are operator/runbook topics (network access, content organization, system admin, procedural drills, meta-reference). Systematic pattern: **encyclopedia and Wikipedia hits appear even when they don't address the query** (e.g., "Canning" → "Canning (name)", "Fire" → "Online encyclopedia"), suggesting the search engine's fallback strategy overweights encyclopedic disambiguation when specific practical manuals don't match well. The two worst failures ("AI assistants" and "Maps") suggest phrase-matching gaps: neither term appears in practical medical/navigation resources the library holds, causing the engine to return contextually distant matches.
