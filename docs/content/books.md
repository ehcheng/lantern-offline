# Canonical Survival / Homesteading / Off-Grid Library — Consolidated Research

Research date: 2026-09-14. Sources: r/preppers threads and derivative lists, homesteading forums (homesteadingtoday.com), bushcraft communities, ham radio (ARRL ecosystem), herbalism lists, TruePrepper/Prepper Press/Common Sense Home aggregations, publisher sites (Hesperian, NCHFP/USDA), Internet Archive.
Prices are approximate street prices (USD, new print, 2025-2026); page counts approximate per edition. Items marked ~ are recalled/estimated, verify at purchase.

Acquisition-path legend:  
- **free-download** — legally free (public domain, gov't publication, or publisher-authorized PDF)
- **buy-print** — in print, buy the physical book (preferred for offline library)
- **buy-ebook** — DRM'd or ebook-only sensible option
- **scan** — out of print or no legal digital copy; acquire used print and scan for the archive

## Consolidation notes (why these books)

Across communities the same core recurs with near-total overlap:

- **r/preppers**: SAS Survival Handbook, Survival Medicine Handbook, Where There Is No Doctor, Prepper's Long-Term Survival Guide, Bushcraft 101, military FMs.
- **Homesteading forums**: Encyclopedia of Country Living (near-universal #1), Ball Blue Book, USDA canning guide, Storey's animal guides, Seymour's Self-Sufficient Life, Backyard Homestead.
- **Bushcraft**: Bushcraft 101 (Canterbury), Mors Kochanski's Bushcraft, SAS Handbook, FM 21-76.
- **Ham radio**: ARRL Handbook, ARRL license manuals, ARRL Emergency Communication Handbook / Operating Manual.
- **Herbalism**: Rosemary Gladstar (Medicinal Herbs; Herbal Recipes for Vibrant Health), Alma Hutchens, Peterson field guides, Samuel Thayer for foraging.
- **Medical**: Where There Is No Doctor/Dentist (Hesperian), Survival Medicine Handbook (Alton), Ditch Medicine (Coffee), Special Operations Forces Medical Handbook.
- **Civilizational-reboot niche**: The Knowledge (Dartnell) — frequently cited as the "big picture" book, not a field manual.

Key structural insight from every list: communities rank **reference density + offline usability** over narrative. Books that are "recipes and tables you consult under stress" (canning guides, WTIND, ARRL Handbook) rank above narrative survival memoirs.

## Acquisition summary

**Free-download bundle (grab first, ~0 cost, covers medicine + canning + military):**

- Hesperian set (WTIND, No Dentist, Where Women Have No Doctor, Book for Midwives): hesperian.org/books-and-resources/
- USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning 2015: nchfp.uga.edu
- FM 21-76 + Ranger Handbook + FM 4-25.11 + Emergency War Surgery + SOF Medical Handbook: archive.org / armypubs.army.mil / fas.org
- Nuclear War Survival Skills (Kearny), Humanure Handbook 3rd ed., USDA Wood Handbook, Gray's Anatomy 1918

**Scan-needed (out of print / defunct publisher):**

- Ditch Medicine (Paladin Press defunct)
- ARRL Emergency Communication Handbook (2006, OOP — or substitute current ARRL Operating Manual)
- Where There Is No Vet (patchy availability)
- Old Merck Manual editions

**Buy-print core order (~$250–300 covers Tier 1 non-free):**
Encyclopedia of Country Living, SAS Survival Handbook, Ball Blue Book, Survival Medicine Handbook 4th, ARRL Technician + General manuals, Bushcraft 101 — then Tier 2 as budget allows (Seed to Seed, Seymour, Thayer x2, Gladstar, Foxfire 1–3, used ARRL Handbook).

## Caveats / risks

- **Editions matter for safety content**: canning (only 2015 USDA / current Ball — older recipes are unsafe), ham license manuals (question pools rotate), Survival Medicine Handbook (4th ed. substantially expanded).
- **Archive.org copies of in-copyright books** (The Knowledge, SAS Handbook) are lending-only, not legal downloads — listed availability above reflects that.
- **Circulating "free PDFs"** of Gladstar, ARRL, Alton books on dokumen.pub/pdfdrive-type sites are pirated; not listed as acquisition paths.
- Page counts/prices approximate; verify at order time.
