# Offline Survival Knowledge Base — Content Requirements by Domain

Research date: 2026-09-14. Target: months-to-years off-grid, served via Kiwix/HTTP from small box to phones over WiFi. Budget >1TB (comfortably fits everything below; full stack estimate ~600–900 GB).

Legend: **[FREE]** free download · **[BUY]** purchase required · **[SCAN]** in-print copyrighted, would need scan/purchase of ebook · sizes approximate.

## Acquisition notes / tradeoffs

- **Free layer first** (~400–600 GB): all Kiwix ZIMs, Survivor Library, CD3WD, Hesperian, WHO/MSF, military manuals, FEMA, OSM+USGS maps, Kolibri channels. This alone is a viable library.
- **Purchase layer** (~$800–1500 print+ebook): the [BUY] items above are mostly still-in-copyright standards with no free equal (Auerbach, ARRL Handbook, Thayer foraging, Storey's guides, Encyclopedia of Country Living, Machinery's Handbook, vehicle-specific manuals). Buy DRM-free PDFs where offered (ARRL, SEI, Hugh Piggott do); otherwise print copies are the honest fallback — paper needs no server.
- **Scan-needed**: only where the ebook is DRM-locked and the content is mission-critical (Oxford Handbook, Art of Electronics). Legal note: scanning owned copies for personal use is a gray area; the owner's call.
- **Format**: prefer ZIM (Kiwix serves to phones over WiFi natively); PDFs served by any static file server; Kolibri for structured education. Keep a manifest with checksums.
- **Refresh cadence**: ZIMs update monthly-quarterly; drug references and Nautical Almanac are the only truly time-sensitive items.
