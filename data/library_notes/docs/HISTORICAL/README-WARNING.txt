*** HISTORICAL DOCUMENTS — DO NOT USE FOR PATIENT CARE ***

Everything in this folder is pre-2010 and kept for background/context only.
Medical practice has changed. These documents contain guidance now known to
be outdated or harmful (old tourniquet doctrine, outdated drug choices and
doses, obsolete resuscitation ratios).

For actual care, use instead:
  - ../medical/tccc/            (TCCC Guidelines 2024 — current trauma standard)
  - ../medical/msf/             (MSF Clinical Guidelines — current)
  - ../medical/who_*            (WHO current references)
  - ../../triage.html           (symptom index, current sources only)

Never take a drug or dose from a HISTORICAL document.
