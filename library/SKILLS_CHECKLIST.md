# Skills practice checklist: one drilled rep each, while it is easy

Content without practice is dead weight. Do each item once for real, while
the grid is up and a mistake costs nothing. Date and initial each line. Redo
yearly.

Print this. Keep it with the runbook. On Lantern it is in the front page
sidebar under "Skills checklist", or type http://library.lan/_app/skills.html.

## Library operation

- [ ] ____ A second household member powers the box on, joins `LIBRARY`, and finds a document alone, with no coaching (runbook Part 1).
- [ ] ____ Fallback drill (runbook Part 2, step 3): power off, unclip the NVMe ribbon, plug the backup SSD into a blue USB port, power on. Confirm the library loads from the SSD. Put the ribbon back.
- [ ] ____ Backup drill (runbook Part 4, item 5): sync the library to the SSD and run the file check. Write the date in the paper notebook.
- [ ] ____ Install Organic Maps and Kiwix on your own phone from tools/android/ (on Lantern: Tools shelf, "Android apps"), copy one map region and one small .zim over, open both with Wi-Fi off.
- [ ] ____ Start the box from both power banks in turn. Note how many hours each one lasts with the router on; write it in runbook Part 1.

## Medical

- [ ] ____ Tourniquet on your own thigh and a partner's arm (high and tight, tighten until the pulse below it is gone). Time yourself: under 60 seconds.
- [ ] ____ Pressure dressing and wound packing on a rolled towel.
- [ ] ____ Walk one full scenario through the symptom triage on a phone until it feels automatic. On Lantern: the red "Someone is hurt or sick" button on the front page, or type http://library.lan/triage.html.
- [ ] ____ Mix oral rehydration solution from the [Where There Is No Doctor recipe](ref:wtnd_dehydration); taste it. On Lantern: front page, Medical shelf, Hesperian guides.
- [ ] ____ Take a first-aid and CPR class. Hands-on beats every PDF in this library.

## Food and water

- [ ] ____ One full pressure-canning run from the [USDA guide](ref:usda_canning), adjusted for your altitude. Eat the result a month later. On Lantern: front page, Food preservation shelf.
- [ ] ____ One water boil and one filter or chemical treatment of pond or rain water. On Lantern: front page, Water shelf.
- [ ] ____ Positively identify 5 local edible plants and their dangerous lookalikes, outdoors, not from the couch. On Lantern: front page, ["Plants, mushrooms & the coast"](ref:plants).
- [ ] ____ Start seeds, transplant, harvest one vegetable, one full cycle. On Lantern: front page, Homestead & garden shelf.

## Power and communications

- [ ] ____ Run the box on battery only for 24 hours; log the bank's charge level at start and end.
- [ ] ____ Solar path: panel to charge controller to battery, confirm it is charging. On Lantern: front page, Energy & power shelf.
- [ ] ____ One ham radio contact, or at minimum program and use FRS/GMRS radios with a family member a mile away. Get the license while it is easy. On Lantern: front page, Communications shelf.
- [ ] ____ Flash and pair two Meshtastic nodes; send a message with Wi-Fi off. On Lantern: Communications shelf, Meshtastic guide.

## Shelter and fire

- [ ] ____ Light a fire with a ferro rod and natural tinder, in wind. On Lantern: front page, Fire safety shelf.
- [ ] ____ One night in your evacuation shelter (tent, tarp, or vehicle) in bad-ish weather. On Lantern: front page, Shelter shelf.
- [ ] ____ Kill the main breaker and the gas valve; everyone in the house finds them in the dark.

When every box is checked, the library is a capability. Until then it is a hard drive.
