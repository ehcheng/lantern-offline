Lantern

Map

Español

The whole planet, streets and buildings, from this box — no internet. This map cannot show where you are.

It has no place search: find a town or street by dragging and zooming until its name shows. Red crosses mark hospitals, clinics, doctors and pharmacies as OpenStreetMap listed them in September 2026, plus Stanford Hospital, which OpenStreetMap has only as an area. Only some hospitals show at the opening view; more appear with each tap on +. A few clinics and doctors show after 2 taps, most of them and the pharmacies after 3, so zoom in before you decide none is near; places close and move, so check before you count on one. For a dot that follows you, use Organic Maps or OsmAnd on an Android phone (files below).

Maps for your phone

For turn-by-turn use in the field, copy a region file to your phone and open it in the free Organic Maps app (Android installer in tools/android/, with install steps in its README). The same regions for OsmAnd are listed second. Files are grouped by state/region.

Nautical charts

US coastal and inland charts (NOAA ENC and RNC) are in maps/noaa_charts/. They need a chartplotter program on a laptop: OpenCPN, Mac and Windows installers in tools/opencpn/ with setup steps in its README.
